Information Theory and Machine Learning Group

School of Electrical Engineering, KAIST (2005 – 2022)

About the Group

The Information Theory and Machine Learning (ITML) Group was active in the School of Electrical Engineering at KAIST from 2005 to 2022 under the supervision of Prof. Sae-Young Chung. Our research focused broadly on information theory, network information theory, coding theory, wireless communications, and machine learning including deep reinforcement learning.
